# PID-XXXX-OC: [Opportunity Title]

## 1. Problem & Context
* **User Pain Point / Market Opportunity:** Describe the specific problem being solved or the market gap being addressed.
* **Cost of Inaction:** What happens if we do nothing? What are the current operational, financial, or engineering costs of the status quo?

## 2. Solution Hypothesis
* **Conceptual Approach:** High-level summary of the proposed direction, product capability, or architectural pivot.
* **Strategic Alignment:** How does this initiative support the company's long-term vision and technical goals?

## 3. Success Metrics & KPIs
* **Quantitative Targets:** Specific, measurable metrics that will define success (e.g., reduce processing latency by 30%, increase conversion rates by X%).
* **Validation Milestones:** Key checkpoints to prove or disprove the hypothesis early.

## 4. Preliminary Feasibility & Risks
* **Technological Uncertainty:** Core technical hurdles, unknown integrations, or model limitations.
* **Business / Operational Risks:** Adoption barriers, compliance hurdles, resource constraints, or cost implications.
