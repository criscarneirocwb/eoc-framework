# PID-XXXX-BR: [Business Requirements Title]

## 1. Business Objectives & Drivers
* **Commercial Goals:** High-level commercial rationale, market expansion, revenue opportunities, or strategic positioning.
* **Cost / ROI Analysis:** Direct financial impacts, anticipated cost savings, infrastructure cost offsets, or development ROI.

## 2. Regulatory & Compliance Constraints
* **Legal Mandates:** External legal requirements (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2, regional data sovereignty, PCI-DSS).
* **Corporate Governance:** Internal policies, infosec rules, auditability, data retention boundaries, and access tiers.
* **Risk Tolerance:** Maximum acceptable risks, legal exposures, and non-negotiable operational red lines.

## 3. Business Acceptance Criteria & SLAs
* **Service-Level Targets:** Uptime guarantees, recovery time objectives (RTO), recovery point objectives (RPO).
* **Acceptance Benchmarks:** Quantifiable baseline criteria the engineering output must satisfy before commercial launch.

## 4. Stakeholders & Approving Authorities
* **Business Owner:** Individual or group accountable for the business outcome.
* **Impacted Units:** Internal teams, operations, customer support, legal, security.
